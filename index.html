03 / OUR MISSION

Not lost.
Remembered.

Mission Afterlife is an educational storytelling project dedicated to exploring the forgotten machines of space exploration. We connect their missions, scientific discoveries and technological legacies.

Our goal is to inspire the next generation of explorers.
